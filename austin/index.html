Keeping Your Austin HVAC System Running Longer

A little routine care makes all the difference between a system that runs for years and one that quits in the middle of a heat wave. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

Take a look at the age of your system. That number tells you a lot. Most well-maintained air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, gas furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is past that range and is also showing rising bills, uneven temperatures, or repeated repairs, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and less stressful than a rushed emergency install when the first heat wave hits.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. The same capacitor left alone for a season can take the compressor with it and turn a minor repair into a full system replacement. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.
