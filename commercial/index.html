Food Service Comfort

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

Restaurants and food-service businesses have climate demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Kitchens generate enormous heat loads while dining rooms must stay comfortable, exhaust hoods pull out conditioned air that makeup air units must replace, and a busy Friday night pushes rooftop units to their limit. Longview HVAC specializes in food-service HVAC, including rooftop package units, split systems, correct tonnage and airflow sizing, makeup air balancing against hood exhaust, and scheduled service that works around your hours.

Many restaurant comfort complaints trace back to air balance. When a kitchen runs negative, it pulls dining-room air in the wrong direction, drags hot or cold outdoor air in through every door, and leaves staff miserable during the rush. We balance makeup air against hood exhaust and document every visit in written reports your landlord and auditors can review.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping your kitchen running and your dining room comfortable.

Inspections & Documentation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Badly installed or neglected HVAC equipment can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and expensive water damage from clogged condensate lines. Those are real risks for a commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Every installation and inspection we perform covers heat exchanger integrity, venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge, condensate drainage, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. Every account gets a service binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Equipment that works hardest, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, is put on a more frequent service schedule.

Fast Response

Commercial Emergency HVAC Response in Longview

When a commercial HVAC system fails, the clock starts immediately. Kitchens get too hot to work, shoppers walk out, server rooms overheat, and tenants start complaining. Calls to our commercial line get a technician rolling fast, throughout Longview and the surrounding area.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. The cause could be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, an ignition or gas valve fault, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board stuck in lockout. Whatever the cause, we diagnose it fast, restore heating or cooling, and document the entire call for your facility files. Afterward, we put maintenance and monitoring in place to keep it from happening again.

Preventive Care

Commercial Scheduled Maintenance Programs

Stopping failures before they happen is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial HVAC equipment. Every Longview HVAC program is customized to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and includes scheduled inspections, coil cleaning, condensate line clearing, filter changes with MERV ratings matched to the space, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal prep before peak demand.

Compared with an emergency replacement, scheduled maintenance costs a fraction as much, and it sharply cuts the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Businesses that commit to a program usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills. Give us a call to discuss a plan for your property and budget.
